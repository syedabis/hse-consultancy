SSGC / SUI SOUTHERN GAS COMPANY

80 KM High-Pressure 42" RLNG Pipeline EPC Delivery

GASCO executed the complete EPC construction for the 80 KM, 42-inch high-pressure RLNG transmission pipeline for SSGC, connecting key national gas distribution headers across Sindh under tight schedule constraints.

The project involved heavy trench excavation in challenging soil terrain, specialized horizontal directional drilling (HDD) river crossings, automatic orbital welding, 100% radiographic NDT testing, and high-pressure hydrostatic testing.

Through comprehensive cathodic protection system installation, precision bending, and seamless valve station integration, GASCO ensured safe, high-capacity gas transmission with zero safety incidents.

Along the 80 KM RLNG pipeline route, GASCO successfully executed complete trenching, pipe stringing, and river crossing infrastructure. By leveraging heavy machinery fleets, automated welding technology, and precision NDT inspection, we enabled continuous national energy delivery for SSGC.

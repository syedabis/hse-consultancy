Proven Risk Advisory Framework

5-Stage HSE & Risk Consulting Methodology

Our structured 5-stage advisory framework ensures rigorous hazard identification, quantitative risk modeling, barrier implementation, and long-term compliance governance across complex energy operations.

01

Initial Hazard Scoping

Comprehensive site evaluations, regulatory baseline mapping, hazard identification, and defining client-specific HSE performance standards.

02

Process Risk Assessment

Facilitating HAZOP, HAZID, and LOPA workshops, consequence modeling, and Quantitative Risk Assessments (QRA).

03

System & Barrier Design

Designing tailored ISO 45001 & 14001 management systems, emergency response plans, and major hazard risk barriers.

04

Audit & Workforce Training

Executing independent site compliance audits, permit-to-work rollouts, and OSHA/NEBOSH aligned leadership safety coaching.

05

Governance & Re-Certification

Ongoing HSE performance tracking, root cause incident analysis (RCA), and annual ISO re-certification audit support.

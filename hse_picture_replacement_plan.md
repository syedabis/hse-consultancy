## 💡 Summary Instructions for Image Uploads

When preparing or generating these images:
1. **Color Palette:** Ensure modern, high-contrast aesthetics (dark slate blue, warm safety amber/yellow `#FFBF43`, crisp white).
2. **PPE Standards:** All personnel in photos MUST wear clean, standard industrial safety gear (white or yellow hard hats, safety glasses, high-vis vests/FRC coveralls, steel-toe boots).
3. **Aspect Ratios:**
   - **Hero Cards / Banners:** 16:9 ratio (e.g., `1920x1080` or `1200x675`)
   - **Feature Showcase Cards:** 4:3 ratio (e.g., `800x600`)
   - **Gallery Marquee Cards:** 4:3 ratio (e.g., `600x450`)
